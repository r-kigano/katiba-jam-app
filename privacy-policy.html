Privacy Policy — Katiba Jam

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

Katiba Jam does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us or sold to anyone.

What the app stores on your device

The app remembers a few preferences directly on your phone or tablet, using standard on-device storage — not a server. This includes:

  • Your chosen text size
  • Whether High Contrast mode is on
  • Whether sound is on or off

None of this ever leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect names, emails, locations, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect any.

Network requests

The app loads two typefaces (Baloo 2 and Atkinson Hyperlegible) from Google Fonts over the internet so the text renders correctly. This is a standard request to Google's font delivery network; it does not carry any information you enter in the app, though your device's IP address is visible to Google as part of any internet request, the same as visiting any website. Google's own privacy policy governs that request: policies.google.com/privacy.

Content and sources

The constitutional text presented in the app is drawn from publicly published sources, primarily Kenya Law (the National Council for Law Reporting). Katiba Jam is an independent educational app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Government of Kenya or any of its agencies.

Children's privacy

Katiba Jam is designed to be usable by all ages, including children, precisely because it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a new feature that touches personal data — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the app's store page.
