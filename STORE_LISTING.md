## Full description

Katiba Jam turns the Constitution of Kenya, 2010 into a colorful, easy-to-read civics companion — built to be just as friendly for a curious student as it is for a grandparent, in Nairobi or in the diaspora.

WHAT'S INSIDE
🎺 We, the People — the Preamble, read aloud with the words lighting up as they're read.
🛡️ Meet the Government — the Legislature (Parliament), the Executive (the President), and the Judiciary explained in plain language, each with its own sound, plus a "checks and balances" seesaw you can play with — and a fourth pillar: Devolution, with all 47 counties blooming onto the screen.
📜 The Bill of Rights Songbook — the biggest rights from Chapter Four as big, clear flashcards, with a plain-words version and the full legal text of each article.
📖 Read the Whole Katiba — all 18 chapters of the Constitution, browsable and searchable, right on your phone.
🎯 Katiba Quiz — questions across seven topics (Preamble, Legislature, Executive, Judiciary, Bill of Rights, Devolution & Counties, History & Founding). Pick your topics and how many minutes you have (10, 15, 20, 25, or 30), then answer as many as you can before time runs out.

BUILT FOR EVERY AGE
- A "Control Booth" with one-tap text size, high-contrast, and sound controls
- Big, legible type (Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for low-vision readers)
- No flashing or strobing effects — every animation is slow and gentle
- No ads, no accounts, no data collection

A great way for classrooms, civic education, harambee groups, or anyone studying for citizenship or public service exams to make the Katiba stick — by reading it, playing with it, and quizzing on it.

Katiba Jam is an independent educational app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Government of Kenya or any of its agencies. The constitutional text used in this app is drawn from publicly published sources, primarily Kenya Law (the National Council for Law Reporting), kenyalaw.org.
